Hotel Booking Dashboard – Step-by-Step Procedure

1.  I opened Power BI Desktop and imported the cleaned hotel booking
    dataset from Excel.
2.  After loading the data, I checked the columns and verified that the
    data types were correct.
3.  I created a new report page and gave the dashboard the title “Hotel
    Booking Dashboard”.
4.  I added slicers for Hotel, Country, Arrival Year, Arrival Month,
    Market Segment, Customer Type, Booking Status, and Deposit Type.
5.  I created KPI cards to display Total Bookings, Total Revenue, Total
    Guests, Average Daily Rate (ADR), and Confirmed vs Cancelled ratio.
6.  I inserted a clustered bar chart to show Bookings by Hotel Type
    (City Hotel and Resort Hotel).
7.  I added a doughnut chart to display bookings by Market Segment.
8.  I created a pie chart to compare Confirmed and Cancelled bookings.
9.  I inserted a column chart to display Monthly Bookings using Arrival
    Month.
10. I created a line chart to analyse the Revenue Trend by month.
11. I added a horizontal bar chart to analyse bookings by Customer Type.
12. I formatted all visuals by adjusting titles, colours, labels,
    borders, and alignment.
13. I arranged the visuals neatly on the report page to improve
    readability.
14. I tested all slicers to ensure they filtered every visual correctly.
15. Finally, I reviewed the dashboard, saved the Power BI report, and
    confirmed that it provides an interactive analysis of hotel
    bookings, revenue, customer types, market segments, and booking
    status.
